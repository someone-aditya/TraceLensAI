05 / RESPONSIBLE SEARCH

Powerful doesn't have to mean invasive.

TraceLens is designed around searching a submitted image, not identifying an unknown private person.

⌕

Specific-image search

Start with the exact image you own or have permission to investigate.

↗

Real returned sources

Result cards point to actual destination URLs supplied by the configured search provider.

◇

No private-person identification

TraceLens is not designed to identify strangers or find unrelated accounts from someone's face.

◌

No fake certainty

Results are leads to review. TraceLens does not invent similarity percentages or claim that every returned page proves misuse.
