ABOUT TRACELENS

Protect your digital image.

TraceLens is a privacy-focused concept designed to help users investigate where a specific photo may have been copied or reposted online.

PRIVACY FIRST

Built around consent.

TraceLens is designed for photos users own or have permission to check. It does not identify unknown private individuals or search for unrelated photos solely by someone's face.
